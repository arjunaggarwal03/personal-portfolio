Chat is good at catching a request before the person has worked out every
detail. The transcript becomes a bad place to keep the durable record once that
request turns into shared work.

The action should say what was proposed, who owns it, what state it is in, what
it will change, and whether it can be reversed. The interface can hide most
intermediate steps while still showing the parts that carry consequences.
